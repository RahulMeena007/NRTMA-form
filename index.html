Guidelines & Policies

How to Apply

Submit your application on the prescribed form with participation fee (Cheque/Cash/Net Banking) to Adv. Prog. Coordinator, R.No.32-G, GM's Bldg., Baroda House, New Delhi-110001 within due dates. Forms are also available with NRTMA Committees across Divisions, Workshops, and the centralised office. You can also apply through our online form.

NRTMA Reserves the Right

  • To increase the participation fee due to unforeseen eventualities.
  • To alter duration of the camp, route, or overnight halts in case of bad weather, roadblocks, or circumstances beyond control.
  • For selection process of participants & rejection of any application without assigning any reason.

Points to Note

  • Every programme includes mountaineering, trekking & adventure activities — not just sight-seeing.
  • Use of intoxicants in any form is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants liable to be sent back at own cost if discipline is infringed.
  • Preference is given to healthy applicants. Must be physically fit and mentally prepared.
  • All activities materialise subject to receiving sufficient response.
  • All participants must carry one photo ID.

Cancellation

Selected participants not willing to attend must inform in writing specifying the reason before 10 days of commencement, otherwise 25% of the fee will be forfeited.

Flagging Ceremony

Before commencement, a flagging-off ceremony is organized by NRTMA during which participants are briefed about the itinerary & trek route.
